The Aetheria

At the heart of the world lies Aetheria, a once-peaceful kingdom protected by an ancient power that keeps the balance between the six realms. But darkness has begun spreading from the Black Dragon's Den, awakening dragons and threatening the lands of Leona, Thornwick, Azharim, Solmara, and Frosthelm. As ancient ruins stir and forgotten secrets emerge, heroes from every corner of the world must rise to face the growing threat. Their journey will determine the fate of Aetheria: will they unite the realms and restore peace, or watch the world fall into darkness?
